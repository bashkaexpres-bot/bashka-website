About Bashka Express

Built truck by truck, not overnight

Bashka Express started as one truck and grew into a full regional fleet — every truck added because the work demanded it, not because a spreadsheet said to. We're based in Eagan, Minnesota, and we run this like a family business, because it is one.

Our managers rotate 24-hour coverage so dispatch is never asleep at the wheel, and every driver goes through the same pre-trip and post-trip inspection process — logged, timestamped, and on file.
